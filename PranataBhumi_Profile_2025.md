## **04  What We Do** 

Five services, deployable independently or as an integrated system. 

## **01  Precision Mapping** 

_Spatial foundation of the estate_ 

- Block polygon mapping from satellite and UAV imagery (10-30m resolution) 

- Legal boundary verification: HGU overlap, protected area buffers 

- Land cover change history: deforestation check 2015 to present 

- Soil type zoning, drainage network, and terrain analysis 

- Audit-ready geodatabase for RSPO, ISPO, and EUDR submissions 

## **02 Vegetation Health Monitoring** 

_Continuous canopy intelligence via satellite_ 

- NDVI, EVI, LAI per block, updated every 10-16 days from Sentinel-2 

- Health classification: optimal, suboptimal, stress, critical (palm-calibrated thresholds) 

- Rainfall accumulation (30/60/90-day), evapotranspiration, and water stress index 

- Automatic alerts when any block crosses a critical threshold 

## **03  Yield Intelligence** 

_Closing the gap between actual and potential FFB_ 

- Regression analysis: which field conditions are statistically linked to your production data 

- Block yield forecast and underperformance diagnosis 

- Effort coefficient: 'this much improvement in NDVI = this much more FFB per hectare' 

- Benchmark: your blocks vs. estate best performers 

## **04 Intervention Advisory** 

_What to do, where, and when_ 

- Multi-layer overlay: vegetation + rainfall + soil + terrain per block 

- Specific prescription per block: fertilisation, drainage, liming, pest management 

- Lag-effect timeline: when to expect yield response after each intervention 

- Priority ranking: highest-impact blocks first, with ROI projection 

## **05 Strategic Replanting** 

_Long-term estate planning on evidence_ 

- Replanting prioritisation by age cohort, LAI decline, and yield trajectory 

- Variety-soil suitability analysis for new planting material selection 

- 5-year phased replanting masterplan with GIS-ready block maps 

- RSPO and EUDR compliant replanting documentation

## **05  How We Work** 

Our method is called Science-Gated Advisory. We do not recommend until the data says we should. 

|**Step**|**What Happens**|**Why It Maters**|
|---|---|---|
|01  Map|Block polygons loaded. Satellite data pulled<br>automatcally per block.|Every analysis is spatally anchored.|
|02  Monitor|EO parameters (NDVI, rainfall, LST, soil<br>moisture) updated every 10-16 days.|Contnuous, not annual.|
|03  Validate|Regression of EO parameters vs. your FFB<br>producton records. Gate: R2 above 0.40.|We only recommend what the data<br>supports.|
|04  Prescribe|Blocks with validated conditons receive<br>specifc, prioritsed interventons.|Targeted acton, not estate-wide<br>guessing.|
|05  Verify|Next harvest cycle compared to pre-<br>interventon baseline.|Clients see the return on every<br>acton.|
